## How I Approach Frontend Development

I don't just focus on making an interface **look right**.

I care about how the product behaves after it ships.

### 01 — Understand

I start by understanding the product, requirements, users, and constraints before jumping into implementation.

### 02 — Structure

I organize the application around reusable components, predictable patterns, and maintainable architecture.

### 03 — Build

I turn designs and requirements into responsive, accessible, production-ready interfaces.

### 04 — Optimize

I look for unnecessary renders, excessive JavaScript, poor loading behavior, layout shifts, and other performance bottlenecks.

### 05 — Refine

I pay attention to the details that separate a functional interface from a polished product.
